# Studiefinanciering and related values, 2026 (NL)

Researched 2026-10-29. Every value was checked against an official source: duo.nl, wetten.overheid.nl, rijksoverheid.nl, belastingdienst.nl, rechtspraak.nl or maastrichtuniversity.nl.
Confidence labels:
- **VERIFIED**: the value is stated literally in an official source.
- **DERIVED**: the value is computed from VERIFIED values with an officially stated formula. It is not stated as such in a source.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no official statement was found. The value is a best estimate.

Amounts are in EUR per month unless the unit says otherwise. "valid from" is the first day the value applies.
HO amounts (except the collegegeldkrediet) change on 1 January. The collegegeldkrediet changes on 1 September, with the studiejaar.
MBO amounts change on 1 January (indexation). The lesgeld supplement in the mbo aanvullende beurs changes on 1 August.


"Andere kinderen" (the €363 deduction) means children without SF who in the AB calendar year are 12 to 18 (not yet 19); or 18 to 30 in vo, (v)so or vavo; or under 18 in mbo with a reisproduct. They must be registered at the parent's address, or stay with the parent at least 3 days a week under co-parenting (DUO-AB, FOLD-HO26).
DUO grants the extra single-parent exemption only when the parent lives "alleen met een kind jonger dan 18 jaar, in Nederland" (DUO-INK).

### 2.2 Formula (Wsf art. 3.8, 3.9, 3.9a, 3.13; DUO form FOLD-HO26)

Per parent p (only parents whose income counts):

```
bruto_korting_p   = rate × (toetsingsinkomen_p[peiljaar] − vrije_voet_p)        rate = 0.136 (HO) | 0.26 (MBO); may be negative
netto_korting_p   = bruto_korting_p − studieschuld_termijnen_p[jaar] − 363 × n_andere_kinderen_p
per_kind_p        = netto_korting_p / n_kinderen_met_AB_p                        (art. 3.13 lid 3: split over the parent's children with SF who applied for AB; children past their prestatiebeurs years or who stopped do not count)
VOB               = max(0, Σ_p per_kind_p / 12)                                   (art. 3.13 lid 1–2: per-parent monthly amounts are SUMMED, so one parent's negative amount offsets the other's; a negative total is set to nil)
aanvullende_beurs = max(0, AB_max − VOB)                                          (art. 3.8; VOB never exceeds AB_max)
```

The DUO form sums annual amounts per child, floors at 0 and then divides by 12. The law divides each parent's amount by 12 first. Both give the same result up to rounding: allow ±€0.01 in tests.

DUO rounds to cents. Its exact rounding step is UNVERIFIED.

Effect of income: each €1,000 of extra parental income lowers the HO AB by 0.136 × 1000 / 12 = €11.33 per month (MBO: €21.67). DERIVED.

Income at which the HO AB reaches 0 (2026; two parents, no study debt, no other children, one child with AB):
`41,500.60 + 491.08 × 12 / 0.136 = €84,831.19` combined toetsingsinkomen. DERIVED.
With 2 children receiving AB the cutoff is €128,161.78 (DERIVED). For 2027 it is €88,236.74 (DERIVED).
MBO 2026 (Jan–Jul, lesgeld-plichtig): €67,831.55 uitwonend and €66,524.48 thuiswonend (DERIVED).

### 5.2 Non-EU students

Nothing in the normal case. A student on a study residence permit is not in the list of groups equated with Dutch nationals (BSF art. 3), so gets no SF and no collegegeldkrediet, and pays instellingscollegegeld.
Exceptions:
- Dutch nationality, or verblijfsvergunning type II–V (DUO-VW).
- Certain type I permits under BSF art. 3: asylum, family member of a Dutch national or a permit holder, humanitarian. Checked with DUO's voorwaardenhulp.
- Non-EU family members of an EU migrant worker probably qualify through EU law (Dir. 2004/38 art. 24). BR-MW and DUO-EU only explicitly cover students with EU/EEA/CH/UK nationality whose own, partner's or parent's work counts. UNVERIFIED.

All other points in 5.2 are VERIFIED.
